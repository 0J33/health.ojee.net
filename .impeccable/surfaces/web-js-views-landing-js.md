## Direction contract

THESIS: A health-tracker page that performs the product — the first screen is a real rep. "Today" card (sleep, readiness, steps, a workout) fed "from your watch or phone", so a stranger sees in one glance it reads your device and tracks training. The three plates of the mark label the three meanings of the name. Refuses the generic dark-hero + icon-cards template.

OWN-WORLD: off-black #0A0908 ground, #16130F panels; bone #F2EDE5 ink; the brand's concentric gold ring-plate (#E7B653/#D29F41) as the recurring motif; sleep-blue #A9BEFF, jade #74C3A1, rose #E27B92 as metric marks. Instrument Sans everywhere; Newsreader only for the name moment. Rooms carry the giant word as art, up to 11.5rem. Imagery = real app tiles (sleep hero with score ring, readiness, steps, cardio, leaderboard, widgets), built from the app's own look, labelled sample.

STORY: a stranger learns rep. reads whatever they wear (sleep, heart, steps, workouts) and scores the day; that the name is repair (recovery), repetition (training), reputation (friends' board); then opens the web app or asks to test the apps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left column — wordmark + "Build your rep." + one sentence naming devices and training + two CTAs (Open the web app / Become a tester) + a quiet "reads from Fitbit, Pixel, Garmin, Apple Watch or your phone" line. Right column — a real Today card: wide sleep tile (7h17m, 97 score ring), readiness, steps, a workout row. Primary action top-left.

FORM: clean premium product page, candidate from the user's "classic product page" choice, raised to Apple-level; seed fd0a381e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
