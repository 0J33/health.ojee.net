Support

Last updated 13 November 2026

Something not working, or a question about rep.? Write to contact@ojee.net. You get an answer from the person who makes rep., usually within a day or two. Say which phone or computer you use and what you tapped, and a screenshot helps.

Signing in

  • rep. signs you in with Google or Apple: Continue with Google or Continue with Apple. There is no password to remember or reset.
  • Had an account from before? Sign in once with your old username and password, under Other ways to sign in, and link Google or Apple. Your username and everything in your account stay as they are, and the old password stops working.
  • You can link both, in Me → Account, so either one signs you in.

Connecting your watch and apps

Open Me → Connections. rep. reads from what you already use: Google Health (Fitbit and Pixel Watch), Health Connect on Android, Apple Health on iPhone, Garmin, Hevy, Strava, smart scales, and Bluetooth heart-rate sensors. The first numbers usually arrive within a minute.

  • Nothing new is showing? Pull down on Today, or tap the sync button at the top. Some watches only send data after their own app has synced.
  • Apple Health: in the iPhone app, Me → Connections → Devices → Watch or tracker → Apple Watch → Connect Apple Health.
  • Hevy: only gym workouts are synced by default. Runs, walks and other cardio can be switched on in Hevy's settings in rep.

Your data

  • Who sees what: Me → Privacy. Sleep, heart, body and other sensitive data are shared with nobody unless you choose.
  • Export everything, or delete your account and all its data: Me → Your data.
  • AI summaries are off until you turn them on in Me → API and AI, which shows what the AI may read and where it goes.
  • The full details are in the Privacy policy.

Someone is bothering you

Every post, comment, photo and profile has Report in its menu, and every profile has Block. See the Community guidelines for how reports are handled.

Not a medical device

rep.'s scores and hints are estimates for fitness and wellness. See a doctor about symptoms, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.
