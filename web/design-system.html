Space, radius, shadow

  • s-1 · 4pxHairline gaps: an icon and its label.
  • s-2 · 8pxGaps inside a group: buttons in a row, a chip row.
  • s-3 · 12pxBetween a card's parts; a row's inner gap.
  • s-4 · 16pxCard padding; the page gutter on a phone (page-pad).
  • s-5 · 20pxBetween sections inside a sheet.
  • s-6 · 24pxBetween page sections.
  • s-7 · 32pxLarge section breaks.
  • s-8 · 44pxThe largest gap: a hero's air.
  • r-s · 10pxSmall insets: an icon tile, a tag.
  • r-m · 14pxInputs, small cards, a floating sheet on a wide screen.
  • r-l · 24pxCards, tiles, groups of rows, sheets' top corners.
  • r-full · 999pxPills and discs: every button, chip, segmented control and floating pill.
  • edgeThe 1px inner edge on cards and buttons that sit on bg, instead of a border.
  • shadow-1A card lifted a little: a toast, a menu.
  • shadow-2Things over the page: sheets, the floating tab bar.
  • shadow-floatFloating pills (live heart rate, whose boards): one shadow for all.
