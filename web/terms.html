Readiness scores, illness hints, summaries and every other number here are estimates made from wearable data. They can be wrong. Do not use them to diagnose or treat anything; talk to a doctor about your health.

Your account

You sign in with an ojee.net account, shared with mtg.ojee.net and dnd.ojee.net. Keep your password to yourself. One account per person.
