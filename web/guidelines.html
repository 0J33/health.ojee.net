Community guidelines

Last updated 11 November 2026

The feed, circles and challenges are for friends training together. Cheer people on, keep it kind, and keep it about the work.

Do

  • Post your own workouts, photos and videos.
  • Ask before posting a photo of someone else, and take it down if they ask.
  • Disagree about training without making it personal.

Don't

  • Harass, threaten, bully, shame bodies, or attack anyone for who they are.
  • Post nudity, sexual content, or anything sexual involving a minor (reported to the authorities).
  • Encourage self-harm, extreme dieting, or dangerous training; or post violence or gore.
  • Share other people's private information, including where they live or train.
  • Spam, sell, advertise, scam, impersonate, or post what isn't yours to post.
  • Cheat on boards and challenges with made-up workouts.

Reporting and blocking

Every post, comment, photo and profile has Report in its menu, and every profile has Block. A blocked person can no longer see your posts, comment on them, or find you, and is not told. You can also write to contact@ojee.net.

How reports are handled

Every report is read by the operator, usually within 48 hours, and the person reported is never told who reported them. Content that breaks these guidelines or the Terms is removed. Depending on how serious it is, the account gets a warning, is suspended, or is closed; illegal content is reported to the authorities. If your content was removed and you think it was a mistake, write to the same address.
