health.ojee.net

Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

health.ojee.net is a small, private health tracker run by Omar Gamal for himself and his friends. It is not a company product and it does not show ads, sell data or track you across other sites. This page says what it keeps and why.

What it collects

  • Your ojee.net account. A username and a password, stored as a one-way hash. It is the same account as mtg.ojee.net and dnd.ojee.net. No email address or real name is asked for.
  • Your profile here. Display name, short bio, time zone, units, goals, and your privacy and notification choices.
  • Health data from Google, only after you connect your Google account. Through the Google Health API the app reads: steps, distance, calories, active minutes, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, skin temperature, sleep, weight, body fat, exercises, and your tracker's name and battery level. It also keeps the Google account's email address so the Connections page can show which account is linked, and the tokens Google issues so it can keep syncing.
  • What you add yourself. Workouts you start or log, sets imported from a Hevy export, weights you enter, and the personal notes you write for summaries.
  • If you turn notifications on. The push address your browser gives the app for that device.
  • If you add your own OpenCode key. The key, stored encrypted.
  • Technical records. A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and short-lived server logs of errors. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

What it writes to Google

Only what you ask for: a weight you enter, and workouts you confirm, log or choose to upload. You can decline that permission when connecting and everything else still works.

How it is used

To show you your own dashboard, trends, workouts and summaries; to work out leaderboards, streaks and records; and to send the notifications you switched on. Nothing else.

Who can see what

  • By default, your activity (steps, active minutes, distance, calories) and workouts appear on the leaderboard and on your profile page, which anyone with the link can open. Sleep, heart and body data are private by default.
  • You can change each category, or make the whole account private, in Me, Privacy. Private data is never copied into the tables the leaderboard and profiles read.
  • The person running the server can technically read the database. He does not look at other people's data except to fix a problem you report.

AI summaries

When you ask for a summary, or have the weekly summary notification on, the app sends a short block of computed statistics (for example average sleep and step totals for the period) and your personal notes to an AI model provided by OpenCode, which writes the text. Raw samples, your name, your username and your Google account are not sent. The app prefers OpenCode models that keep no data and never uses the ones OpenCode describes as logged or used for training. If you never open Summary and leave the weekly summary off, nothing is sent.

Google user data

health.ojee.net's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, Google data is used only to provide the features you see in the app, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI models.

Where it is kept and for how long

On one server run by Omar Gamal, in a separate database per person. It is kept until you delete it. Connections to the site are encrypted.

Your choices

  • Export. Me, Your data, Export gives you everything as one file.
  • Disconnect Google. Me, Connections. This revokes the app's access at Google; your history here stays until you delete it. You can also remove the app at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Delete. Me, Your data, Delete my health data removes your health database, your Google tokens, your profile, your leaderboard rows, your notification devices and any saved key, immediately. The shared ojee.net login itself stays, because the other ojee.net sites use it; write to the address below to have that removed too.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes and contact

If this page changes in a way that matters, the app will say so. Questions, or a request to delete everything: contact@ojee.net.
